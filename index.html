Speak once. Get it typed, rewritten, translated, or read back.

FewType puts four everyday things in one small Windows app: talk instead of typing, make your words sound the way you want, translate on the fly, and have ebooks read aloud in a natural voice. It's free and open source — you connect the AI services you choose. No subscription, no key included.

Who is FewType for?

Three scenarios, three different kinds of people. You'll probably recognize yourself in at least one.

🎙️

Voice typing — talk instead of typing

Press a global hotkey anywhere (Ctrl+Win or double-press Ctrl), speak, and your words land where your cursor is.

  • Chat fast in messages and AI conversations — no more typing it all out
  • You care how your replies sound — pick a writing style and let it rewrite your words before they land
  • Speak one language, output another — translation goes straight into the text box
📖

Ebook reading — let your ears do the work

A browser extension reads ebooks aloud (Google Play Books, Koodo Reader, EPUB readers). A natural voice, not a robot — sentences flow continuously.

  • You want a human-sounding voice and refuse robotic text-to-speech
  • Long focused reading tires your eyes — pair your eyes with your ears
  • You read during fragments of the day — commute, chores, anywhere your eyes are busy
🎧

Long-text speech — for creators

Paste a long script, pick a voice, get a natural-sounding audio file. A companion feature, but a lifesaver for people who produce spoken content.

  • Voice-over creators who don't want to record every long script themselves
  • Digital presenters and virtual hosts that need clean speech audio
  • Podcast drafts, video voice-over, sample audiobooks, listening practice materials

What's inside

The essentials, in one glance.

🔑 Your keys stay on this machine

API keys live in a local config; the installer contains no keys. Copy or re-pack the app and a security warning appears. Audio and transcriptions are never written to logs.

🌱 Small and light

Low memory use, quiet in the background, a small installer. All the features, none of the bloat.

🌍 Speak one, get another

Chinese, English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean — say it, and it's translated for you.

⚡ No waiting when no translation is needed

Same language in and out? It goes straight to your screen.

🧩 Ebook reading included

The browser reader extension ships with the app — Chrome and Edge.

Open source — and honest about limits

FewType is an indie project. It doesn't pretend to be a mature team product, and that's part of the deal.

What you should know

  • You supply the AI keys. FewType is free software that doesn't sell access — you sign up for the speech, translation and voice services yourself (most major providers work; setup steps are in the README). No key is included or bundled with the app.
  • Free, open source (MIT), no account, no tracking. The code, the builds, the roadmap — all public.
  • Not code-signed. The note below explains what to expect on first run.
  • Built and maintained by one developer, driven by real use and GitHub issues. Scope is honest: voice typing, ebook reading, long-text speech.
⚠️ About the Windows first-run warning
FewType is a free, open-source personal project and doesn't yet pay for expensive enterprise code signing. On first install, Windows SmartScreen may show a warning. It's safe: click "More info" → "Run anyway". All your data and keys stay on this machine — the program never records your raw dictation audio.
